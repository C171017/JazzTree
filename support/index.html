JazzTree for iOS

Support

JazzTree is an offline-first guide to jazz lineage, genres, and listening paths. Here are answers to the most common questions.

Does JazzTree require an account?

No. JazzTree has no account or sign-in. Your preferences and listening-path progress stay on your device.

Why did an album open in another app?

JazzTree is a guide rather than a music player. Album buttons open your chosen external service: Spotify, Apple Music, or NetEase Cloud Music. Availability varies by service and region.

Can I use JazzTree offline?

The complete editorial guide is bundled with the app and works offline. Opening a streaming destination requires the selected music service and an internet connection.

How do I change the language or appearance?

Open the More tab to choose English or Simplified Chinese and select the system, dark, or light appearance.

How do I reset listening progress?

Open Paths, select a listening route, and use Clear Progress after you have marked at least one record as heard.

Report a correction or problem

When reporting an editorial correction, include the genre or album name and the source supporting the change. For a technical problem, include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that reproduce it.
