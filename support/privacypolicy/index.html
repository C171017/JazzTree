JazzTree for iOS

Privacy Policy

JazzTree is designed as a private, account-free listening guide. It does not collect personal data, show advertising, or track you.

Information JazzTree stores

The app stores your appearance, language, preferred streaming service, gentle-listening setting, and listening-path progress locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to JazzTree or used to identify you.

Information JazzTree collects

JazzTree does not collect personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, health information, or usage history. The app does not require or provide a JazzTree account.

External music services

When you choose to open an album, JazzTree may open Spotify, Apple Music, or NetEase Cloud Music. If a direct Apple Music destination is unavailable, the app may send album and artist search terms to Apple’s public iTunes Search service after you tap the link. Your interaction with those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Because JazzTree does not receive your information, it has no personal information to retain or delete. You can clear individual listening paths inside the app. Removing the app clears its locally stored preferences and progress according to iOS behavior.

Children’s privacy

JazzTree does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other users.

Changes to this policy

If JazzTree’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the change is released.

Privacy questions

Contact JazzTree through the support page.
