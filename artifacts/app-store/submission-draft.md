### Description

JazzTree is a visual listening guide to jazz history. Explore 39 genres, see how they descend from, react against, and revive one another, and find about 350 records selected to make those relationships audible.

EXPLORE THE LINEAGE

Follow jazz across time in three complementary views: a focused rail, historical spans, and a full influence map. Filter connections by harmony, rhythm, form, instrumentation, improvisation, timbre, repertoire, social context, and technology.

BROWSE 39 GENRES

Search and sort genre profiles with concise histories, musical traits, key figures, contested boundaries, and gateway, core, and deep-listening albums.

FOLLOW CURATED PATHS

Take three ordered listening routes. Each step explains why one record follows another, and your progress stays on your device.

LISTEN IN YOUR SERVICE

Open albums in Spotify, Apple Music, or NetEase Cloud Music. JazzTree does not require an account.

BUILT FOR CAREFUL EXPLORATION

- About 350 albums
- English and Simplified Chinese
- Light and dark appearances
- Offline editorial content
- Confidence notes for uncertain discographic details
- No ads and no JazzTree account

Genre boundaries are arguments, not facts. JazzTree shows where scholars disagree and makes its editorial uncertainty visible.

## Review notes

JazzTree does not require sign-in. All editorial data is bundled and remains available offline.

Album buttons open external destinations for Spotify, Apple Music, or NetEase Cloud Music. If a curated Apple Music URL is unavailable, the app may query Apple's public iTunes Search API after the user taps the link.

User preferences and listening-path progress are stored locally on the device. JazzTree has no user-generated content, advertising, analytics, purchases, subscriptions, or account functionality.
